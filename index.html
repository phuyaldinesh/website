RESEARCH INTERESTS

Soil scientist focused on sustainable nutrient management for agricultural and environmental systems, with research expertise spanning phosphorus dynamics, biological nitrification inhibition, and climate-smart agriculture. Current work centers on refining phosphorus diagnostics across diverse soil orders to support state and federal Best Management Practices and protect water quality. Long-term research goal: develop science-based, regionally calibrated frameworks that improve nutrient-use efficiency, reduce environmental losses, and advance food and water security.
